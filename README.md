STATLAS — a national statistics portal for China: headline indicators, population, economy, trade, energy and livelihoods, every figure labelled with its source and year. Data: National Bureau of Statistics 2024 Statistical Communiqué, General Administration of Customs, World Bank WDI, and provincial bureaus. All charts are hand-written inline SVG; the site is static files with zero network requests and works on desktop and phone.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080.
